Overview

ETHOBOT 3.2 implements the proposal's persona-driven dilemma dialogue: pre-service teachers in CAT 100 think through a real classroom dilemma while ETHOBOT acts as a Socratic facilitator. Stakeholder personas (teacher, student, parent, administrator) can join the conversation to surface viewpoints the learner has not yet considered. The system records every turn, persona call, recommendation, and pre/post position change for analysis.

The system runs in two experimental conditions:

Learner-directed (LD)

  • Persona panel cards are always clickable.
  • The learner decides who joins and when.
  • ETHOBOT does not signal which persona is most relevant.

AI-recommended (AR)

  • Cards are disabled until ETHOBOT recommends.
  • From turn 4 onward, three rules fire on reasoning gaps.
  • 2-turn cooldown between recommendations; not-yet-called personas take priority.

Deployment & URLs

Each participant gets a personalized URL. The pid parameter becomes the participant identifier in every logged event.

https://ethobot32.vercel.app/cat100?condition=ar&scenario=b&pid=P002&course=CAT100-Spring26

LLM provider — OpenRouter

CAT 100 chat goes through the Vercel Edge function api/cat100-chat.ts, which proxies to OpenRouter with a fallback chain (google/gemini-2.0-flash-001 → google/gemini-flash-1.5 → openai/gpt-4o-mini). The key is server-side only — students never see it.

Required Vercel project env vars:

  • OPENROUTER_API_KEY — secret, server-only. Powers the CAT 100 dialogue.
  • VITE_SUPABASE_URL, VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY — baked at build time; needed for cat100_events persistence.

Cost per session (12 LLM calls × ~500 tokens, Gemini 2.0 Flash): ~$0.01. A 50-participant pilot runs under $0.50 total.

Learner-directed Journey LD

The 15 phases below trace the proposal's worked LD example: a learner who starts with support / 80% / [Safety, Accountability] and ends with unsure / 50% / [Privacy, Fairness, Safety] after talking to two of the four personas.

Step 01

Intro — dilemma context panel

Scenario text, key actors, core ethical tension, guiding question, and persona identity cards (preview).

The learner reads the dilemma in full before seeing any chat. The four persona identity cards are visible but disabled at this stage — they preview who could join the conversation later.

Step 02

Pre-form — empty

Initial position input module, before the learner records anything.

Three required widgets: stance (support / oppose / unsure), confidence slider (0–100%), and value priorities (1–4 of six ISTE-aligned values). Submit stays disabled until stance and at least one value are selected.

Step 03

Pre-form — filled (proposal example)

support / 80% / [Safety, Accountability].

This is the exact starting position from the proposal's worked example (LD section). The submit button enables once the form is valid.

Step 04

Facilitator opening — reflects the learner's stance

ETHOBOT opens the chat with a Socratic question grounded in the just-recorded position.

The opening message reflects both stance and at least one value priority back to the learner before asking what feels most compelling. This is the first verifiable signal that the pre-form data is wired into the dialogue context.

Proposal text: "You said you support adopting the tool. What feels most compelling about that to you?"

Step 05

Probe 1 — assumption surfacing

After the learner's first reply, ETHOBOT probes what would have to be true for the claim to hold.

Step 06

Probe 2 — counterfactual

ETHOBOT escalates with a counterfactual when the learner stays in a single frame.

Step 07

Jordan (Student persona) joins

The learner clicks Jordan's card; persona enters with a self-introduction.

Jordan's identity card transitions to the active state (orange border in screenshot). The persona's self-introduction is voice-consistent with Jordan's style guide: a middle-school student, first-person, slightly hesitant, observational.

Step 08

Mini-dialogue — turn 1

The learner replies; Jordan responds in character. Mini-dialogue length is 2 turns by default.

Step 09

Jordan exits — facilitator returns with a summary

After the second exchange, Jordan's card flips to completed and ETHOBOT automatically streams a 2–3 sentence facilitator return that connects Jordan's contribution to the learner's earlier reasoning.

This handoff is fully automatic — the learner does not need to dismiss the persona or trigger the facilitator return.

Step 10

Mr. Park (Parent persona) joins

The learner picks a second persona; once-called personas (Jordan) cannot be re-opened.

Jordan's card now reads "Spoke with you earlier" and has no Open button — the proposal's "personas not yet called take priority" rule is enforced structurally in the UI.

Step 11

Mr. Park exits — facilitator return

After two more exchanges, Mr. Park exits and ETHOBOT names what he contributed (consent, authority).

Step 12

Post-form — empty

After "End dialogue," the closing-position module appears with the same widgets as the pre-form.

A helper line shows where the learner started ("Compare to where you started: SUPPORT at 80%") so they have an anchor while recording the closing position.

Step 13

Post-form — filled

unsure / 50% / [Privacy, Fairness, Safety] — the proposal's exact closing position.

Step 14

Pre/Post comparison

Stance shift (softened), confidence delta (-30), values added [privacy, fairness], values removed [accountability].

The classification is computed by services/stancePrePost.ts. The four shift labels are same, softened, sharpened, reversed — designed for the proposal's confidence calibration assessment.

Step 15

Debrief

Final summary screen: initial vs closing position, full delta, exit to ETHOBOT root.

AI-Recommended Journey AR

In the AR condition the learner cannot freely click persona cards; ETHOBOT surfaces a recommendation only when one of three reasoning-gap rules fires (and only after turn 4, with a 2-turn cooldown). The four phases below show the moment of trigger.

Step 16

Intro — same as LD

Identical dilemma context panel; URL parameter condition=ar only changes downstream behavior.

Step 17

Chat opening — cards disabled

Persona panel reads "ETHOBOT will surface a persona when relevant". All four cards are disabled.

Step 18

Rule 1 fires — non-teacher persona highlighted

After four turns referencing only the teacher, Rule 1 (single-stakeholder) recommends Jordan.

The recommendation lands as a regular ETHOBOT chat message with a one-sentence rationale plus an inline "Open Jordan →" button. In the panel, Jordan's card flips to highlighted (red ring) while the others stay disabled.

Trigger logic: services/personaRecommender.ts — gating turn ≥ 4 AND (currentTurn − lastRecommendationTurn) ≥ 2; preference for not-yet-called personas.

Step 19

Open accepted — persona enters

Click "Open Jordan →"; recommendation clears, Jordan's card transitions highlighted → active, mini-dialogue begins.

The system records the time between recommendation surface and click as persona_open_latency_ms. From here the mini-dialogue mechanics are identical to the LD condition.

SQL Recipes

Three pre-built views cover the proposal's analysis questions. Run them from the Supabase SQL editor (service-role).

Persona-call patterns

SELECT participant_id, condition, persona_id, trigger_rule,
       open_latency_ms, turn_number, timestamp
FROM   v_persona_call_patterns
WHERE  course = 'CAT100-Spring26'
ORDER  BY participant_id, turn_number;

Pre/Post deltas (one row per session)

SELECT participant_id, condition,
       initial_stance, closing_stance, stance_shift,
       initial_confidence, closing_confidence, confidence_delta,
       values_added, values_removed,
       session_duration_seconds
FROM   v_pre_post_deltas
WHERE  course = 'CAT100-Spring26';

Vocabulary emergence over time

SELECT participant_id, condition, term, first_turn, persona_just_exited
FROM   v_vocabulary_emergence
WHERE  course = 'CAT100-Spring26'
ORDER  BY participant_id, first_turn;

Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeFix
Participant info banner reads cat100-xxxx (anonymous) URL is missing ?pid=... Resend the participant their personalized URL.
Recommendation never fires in AR Gate not yet open (turn < 4) OR every reasoning gap is already covered (multiple stakeholders + multiple value lenses + at least one conditional/evidence) Expected — recommendations are conservative by design. Watch the v_persona_call_patterns view to confirm.
Persona stays in character even when the learner asks "are you AI?" The persona system prompt enforces character consistency; this is the proposal's intent. Document this in the IRB consent so participants know they're talking to AI in voice.
Recommendation fires but Open button does nothing Persona may already be in completed state from a prior call Click another card; the engine prefers not-yet-called personas, so this is rare.
Logs show in Sheets but not Supabase Anon-insert RLS policy missing on cat100_events Re-run supabase/cat100_schema.sql.
